A chat window that drops into any webpage. Bake your OpenRouter key in below and the bookmark works on the current page — it can read the page or your text selection, take a screenshot when it loads a page, and adapts its tone a bit depending on what kind of site you're on.

1. Build your bookmarklet

Just click build — leave the field blank and we'll generate a fresh, small OpenRouter key for you automatically. Already have your own key you'd rather use? Paste it in first. Either way it's embedded straight into the bookmark link on your device — nothing is sent anywhere else, and no key is shared or stored on this page.

  1. Paste your key above first

    Each person builds their own bookmarklet from their own key — the button above updates itself as soon as you click "Build my bookmarklet."

  2. Show your bookmarks bar

    Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + B in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox, if it isn't already showing.

  3. Drag the ClassGPT button up there

    It's a real link — drag it straight into the bookmarks bar. On phones, use the browser's "add bookmark" flow and paste the code from the box below instead.

  4. Click it on any site

    It opens straight into the chat, ready to go, every time. Click the bookmark again (or Ctrl+Shift+G, if that's on) to show or hide it without losing your chat.

  5. Explore the icons in the header

    📁 switches between saved threads, ⚙ opens settings (model, features), ✕ closes. Inside the message box, 📄 reads the page or your current selection (and grabs a screenshot too, unless you've turned that off), 🌐 translates whatever's selected, and 🎤 is voice input.

What's in it

Live streaming repliesResponses type themselves in as they're generated instead of appearing all at once.
Site-aware toneAdjusts its tone a bit depending on the kind of site you're on — docs sites get technical, social sites get casual, and so on.
Page screenshotWhen you load page context, it also grabs a screenshot so the model can see layout/visuals a text extract would miss (needs a vision-capable model).
Multiple threadsKeep separate conversations per topic and switch between them from the 📁 icon.
Pin messagesPin any message so it stays visible in a bar above the chat as it scrolls.
RegenerateNot happy with a reply? Regenerate it in place without retyping your question.
Read page or selection📄 reads your current text selection if you have one, otherwise the whole page.
Image inputPaste or drag an image into the chat and ask about it (needs a vision-capable model).
Translate selection🌐 detects the language of whatever's selected and translates it to your browser's language.
Model pickerDropdown of presets or a custom model id. Defaults to GPT-4o mini.
Markdown, cost estimate, voice input, export, insert-into-pageAll toggleable from settings.

Drag-and-drop not working (some mobile browsers strip it)? Copy this into a new bookmark's URL field manually — build your keyed version above first, then copy from here: building...

A key baked into the bookmark link is stored in plain text inside your browser's bookmarks — fine on a personal device, but don't share the link or sync it somewhere untrusted. It's sent straight from your browser to OpenRouter and never passes through us. Each visitor bakes in their own key locally; this page doesn't collect or see anyone's key.

Reading a page/selection, translating, and inserting text are there to help you understand material and move your own words faster — not to auto-answer graded quizzes or assignments. It's built and prompted with that boundary in mind, and it only acts when you click the button for it.
